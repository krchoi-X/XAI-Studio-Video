# Rio — Character DNA v1

Status: active character candidate
Privacy: private creative asset
Updated: 2026-09-09

## Core identity

Rio is an original young adult East Asian man in his early 20s with a friendly, youthful face and a compact athletic body. His defining contrast is that he looks approachable and harmless in ordinary life, but can become unexpectedly intimidating when serious.

Visual baseline:
- age range: approximately 21–23
- moderate height, around 175–178 cm
- compact athletic build
- functional muscle rather than bodybuilding bulk
- shoulders should remain moderate, not exaggeratedly wide
- youthful soft oval face
- smooth, natural jawline
- calm, warm eyes
- friendly, easygoing first impression
- short dark textured hair
- small single earring as a persistent signature detail

Overall impression:
- grounded
- active
- approachable
- kind at first glance
- physically capable without looking like a stereotypical fighter
- restrained hidden intensity beneath the surface

Identity guardrail:
- avoid bodybuilder proportions
- avoid oversized shoulders or an extreme V-taper
- avoid thug, gangster, or overt martial-arts stereotypes
- avoid flashy jewelry or multiple large earrings
- avoid highly stylized idol hair
- do not make him permanently stern or aggressive

## Facial identity

Rio's face should remain visibly softer and younger than Jun's.

Key facial anchors:
- soft oval facial structure
- relatively short-to-moderate face length
- smooth jawline rather than a sharply cut angular jaw
- warm, calm eyes
- neutral-to-gentle outer eye corners
- natural nose and lips
- friendly resting expression

The face should be realistic and believable rather than excessively polished. Small asymmetries and natural skin texture are desirable.

Anger/serious mode:
- do not radically redesign the face
- reduce expression rather than exaggerating rage
- gaze becomes focused
- mouth becomes still
- overall facial softness remains structurally the same, but the emotional reading changes sharply

## Hair

Direction:
- short dark hair
- practical, lightly textured cut
- natural fringe or subtle forward texture
- easy-care and movement-friendly

Situational variation:
- casual: slightly messy
- after exercise: lightly damp or disordered
- formal occasions: minimally styled, still recognizably Rio

## Signature earring

Rio always wears a small single earring.

Visual rule:
- one ear only
- small and understated
- simple enough to plausibly wear every day
- not flashy luxury jewelry
- should remain visible when framing allows

Current story status:
- the earring is a canonical visual identity marker
- deeper supernatural or technical meaning is not yet fully locked

Provisional lore hook:
- the earring may later function as a limiter, suppressor, seal, stabilizer, or concealment device related to Rio's hidden ability
- removing or disabling it could become a visual shorthand for releasing suppressed capability
- this is intentionally provisional so the final mechanism can be integrated with the wider story world rather than forcing the worldbuilding too early

Production implication:
Because the earring may gain narrative importance later, its size, side, and basic shape should be kept consistent once a canonical reference image is approved.

## Physique and movement

Rio is athletic because he enjoys movement and trains frequently, not because he pursues maximum muscle size.

Physical baseline:
- moderate height
- compact athletic proportions
- moderate shoulders
- toned arms and legs
- relatively low body fat
- functional core strength
- strong balance and quick weight transfer

Reference body types:
- climber
- gymnast
- martial artist
- football/futsal player

Avoid:
- heavyweight fighter silhouette
- bodybuilding chest and deltoids
- extreme six-pack emphasis
- exaggerated vascularity

## Personality

Rio is basically friendly, trusting, active, and action-oriented.

Behavioral baseline:
- likes physical activity
- tends to act before overanalyzing
- adapts quickly in practical situations
- dislikes sitting still for too long
- generally sociable and easy to approach
- rarely becomes openly angry

Core contrast:
When Rio is truly angry or enters a serious threat-response state, he does not become loud. He becomes quieter, more focused, and more frightening precisely because his usual warmth disappears.

## Hidden martial tradition

Rio has learned an old, concealed martial tradition from a family line, private teacher, or similarly restricted lineage.

Current design principle:
- do not finalize the historical name yet
- keep the lineage open until the larger worldbuilding is established
- martial logic should feel physically plausible with a slight layer of mystery

Technical movement language:
- balance breaking
- joint control
- redirecting incoming force
- short-range body mechanics
- efficient weight transfer
- minimal wasted motion

Visual direction:
- not flashy
- not acrobatic for spectacle alone
- short exchanges
- opponents may appear to lose balance before understanding what happened
- Rio should look unusually calm rather than theatrically aggressive

The preferred tone is realistic biomechanics plus restrained mystery, rather than wire-fu or exaggerated fantasy martial arts.

## Wardrobe system

### Casual default

Direction: comfortable, movement-friendly everyday wear.

Typical pieces:
- jeans
- plain T-shirt
- hoodie
- sweatshirt
- training pants
- joggers
- sneakers

### Training

Typical pieces:
- simple performance T-shirt
- lightweight athletic top
- jogger pants
- track jacket
- clean training shoes

The styling should remain casual and functional rather than gym-influencer oriented.

### Formal exception

Rio may wear a suit for specific story occasions, but it should feel slightly unfamiliar or uncomfortable to him.

Narrative contrast:
- formalwear can reveal that he looks unexpectedly composed when dressed properly
- his body language should still retain a subtle sense that he prefers clothes he can move freely in

## Expression and performance

Default emotional register:
- relaxed
- friendly
- gentle
- open
- quietly amused

Secondary expressions:
- curiosity
- physical enthusiasm
- mild embarrassment
- playful confidence

Serious/anger register:
- expression becomes neutral rather than exaggerated
- eyes become sharply focused
- speech and movement become economical
- tension should come from stillness and control

## Base prompt anchor

Rio, an original young adult East Asian man in his early 20s, with a compact athletic build, moderate height, and functional muscle from regular training rather than bodybuilding bulk. He has a gentle youthful face with a soft oval structure, smooth jawline, calm warm eyes, natural realistic features, and an approachable, friendly presence. Beneath that softness there is a subtle hidden intensity, as if he could become quietly intimidating when serious. He has short dark textured hair with a casual practical look, and he always wears a small subtle single earring as a signature detail. Overall he feels grounded, active, kind at first glance, and secretly dangerous beneath the surface, not flashy, not overly broad-shouldered, and not stylized like a fighter stereotype.

## Production rule

Rio should eventually become reference-image-driven rather than repeatedly rediscovered from text alone. Once a canonical face is approved, future generations should preserve:
1. soft youthful facial structure
2. eye shape and warm default gaze
3. short hair silhouette
4. compact athletic body proportion
5. signature single earring

Wardrobe, hairstyle neatness, expression, and training state may vary by scene while these core anchors remain stable.
