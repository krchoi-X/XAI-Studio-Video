# Jun — Character DNA v1

Status: active character candidate
Privacy: private creative asset
Updated: 2026-09-09

## Core identity

Jun is an original young adult East Asian man in his mid-20s with a tall, lean silhouette and a distinctly intellectual, composed presence.

Visual baseline:
- age range: approximately 25–27
- tall, around 184–186 cm
- long-limbed, slender, flexible physique
- not bulky, not heavily muscular, not broad-shouldered in a bodybuilding sense
- slightly long and slim face
- subtly prominent cheekbones as a key identity feature
- clean, refined jawline without an exaggerated V-shape
- narrow, sharp eyes with slightly lifted outer corners
- straight, defined eyebrows
- refined straight nose
- natural, understated lips
- dark black or very dark brown medium-long layered hair
- hair may softly cover or frame the ears and forehead
- sometimes wears thin metal-frame glasses

Overall impression:
- intelligent
- elegant
- reserved
- observant
- slightly severe at first glance
- modern and believable rather than glamorous or idol-like

Identity guardrail:
- avoid generic K-pop idol styling
- avoid exaggerated CEO-model glamour
- avoid bulky shoulders, thick neck, bodybuilder proportions, or overt macho styling
- avoid overly soft baby-face features
- cheekbones should remain subtly visible enough to help facial recognition across generations
- glasses are optional and situational, not a permanent requirement

## Facial identity

Jun's face should derive its character primarily from bone structure and eye shape rather than accessories.

Key facial anchors:
- slightly elongated face
- visible but not extreme cheekbone definition
- relatively narrow lower face
- horizontally elongated eyes
- subtly lifted outer eye corners
- calm, analytical gaze
- relatively straight brow line

The face should remain attractive but not polished into a generic perfect AI male face. Small asymmetries and realistic skin texture are desirable.

## Hair

Direction:
- dark, slightly long male hairstyle
- medium-length layered cut
- natural side part or loose front strands
- neat enough for professional settings but never helmet-like or excessively styled

Situational variation:
- work: cleaner and more controlled
- home / yoga / pilates: slightly loose or disordered
- windy/outdoor scenes: strands may naturally cross the forehead

## Glasses system

Jun sometimes wears thin metal-frame glasses.

Good situations:
- reading
- document review
- computer work
- research
- analysis-heavy office scenes

Narrative/visual function:
- glasses reinforce his analytical mode
- without glasses, his naturally sharp eyes should become more noticeable
- do not make glasses his only identity marker

## Physique and movement

Jun's body should feel trained through control, flexibility, posture, and core strength rather than hypertrophy.

Physical baseline:
- long limbs
- narrow-to-moderate shoulders
- slim waist
- subtle muscle tone
- flexible posture
- clean alignment through neck, shoulders, and spine

Training background:
- pilates
- yoga

Training intent:
- posture
- core stability
- flexibility
- concentration
- stress management

Avoid turning him into a visibly shredded fitness model. His physique should look especially good in tailored clothing because of proportion and posture, not muscular mass.

## Personality

Jun is quick-thinking, logical, analytical, and observant.

Behavioral baseline:
- processes problems structurally
- plans before acting
- good at complex reasoning
- composed in professional situations
- emotionally restrained but not cold
- tends to take mistakes seriously

Core contrast:
Jun occasionally makes absurdly ordinary mistakes despite being highly competent in complex situations.

Examples:
- forgets where he placed coffee
- prepares every document but forgets a pen
- stands in an elevator without pressing a floor button
- searches for his glasses while already wearing them

The humor works best when Jun himself remains completely serious about the mistake.

## Wardrobe system

### Professional default

Direction: restrained modern tailoring.

Typical pieces:
- charcoal suit
- navy suit
- white shirt
- pale blue shirt
- fine-gauge knit
- tailored trousers
- long wool coat
- minimal leather shoes
- slim metal watch

Tie policy:
- optional rather than constant

Visual principle:
- long vertical lines should emphasize his height and elegant proportions

### Casual / home

Typical pieces:
- thin T-shirt
- lightweight knit
- relaxed trousers
- jogger pants
- simple slippers

The contrast between his polished work appearance and relaxed home styling is useful for character scenes.

### Pilates / yoga

Typical pieces:
- fitted but non-compressive training top
- simple long-sleeve athletic top
- tapered training pants
- minimal studio footwear or barefoot where appropriate

Avoid stereotypical gym-body presentation.

## Expression and performance

Default emotional register:
- calm
- thoughtful
- observant
- mildly serious
- restrained confidence

Secondary expressions:
- subtle irritation
- analytical concentration
- quiet amusement
- unexpectedly obvious confusion when he makes a simple mistake

This transition from composed intelligence to visible everyday confusion is an important part of his performance identity.

## Base prompt anchor

Jun, an original young adult East Asian man in his mid-20s, tall and lean with a long-limbed, slender, flexible physique, not bulky and not heavily muscular. He has a slightly long slim face, a clean jawline, subtly prominent cheekbones, sharp narrow eyes with slightly lifted outer corners, straight defined brows, a refined straight nose, and natural understated lips. His expression is calm, thoughtful, observant, and quietly serious, with a subtle hint of occasional awkwardness beneath the composed exterior. He has dark black or very dark brown slightly long layered hair, neat but natural, with soft strands around the forehead and ears. He sometimes wears thin metal-frame glasses. Overall he feels intelligent, elegant, modern, believable, reserved, and original, not flashy, not rugged, and not idol-like.
