# Harim — White Studio Identity Test

Version: 0.2

## Purpose

This is a diagnostic identity prompt, not merely a beauty portrait.

It is designed to test whether the model reproduces Harim's **proportion logic** without making her look vertically stretched or reverting to an ordinary average-height silhouette.

Default hair state: **Hair State A — High Ponytail**

## Full-body standing prompt

Harim, a young adult Korean woman in her early 20s, with a refined slightly cat-like beauty and a tall-looking, elegant long-lined silhouette. She has a small slightly elongated oval face, defined dark eyebrows, almond-shaped dark-brown eyes with subtly upturned outer corners, a refined straight nose, a small mouth with softly full lips, and a slim clean jawline. Her expression is calm, cool, and quietly striking.

Her body has a relatively small head-to-body impression, long neck, relatively narrow shoulders, compact upper torso, small ribcage appearance, slim long arms, narrow defined waist, flat soft lower abdomen, small-to-moderate natural bust, a pelvis slightly broader than average for an East Asian woman without exaggeration, a graceful waist-to-pelvis transition, subtle feminine hip roundness, and long straight legs with slim but healthy thigh volume. She should look slender and elongated, but never skeletal, bony, or artificially stretched.

Her default hairstyle is a high ponytail tied near the crown/upper back of the head, with soft natural crown volume, thin face-framing strands, light wispy fringe or side-bangs, and a partially visible forehead.

Create a photorealistic full-body identity portrait in a clean seamless white studio under bright soft artificial lighting. Use a natural standing pose that clearly shows her small/light upper-body impression, narrow waist, subtle hip line, and long lower-body proportion.

She wears a fitted short-sleeve crop top or fitted sleeveless top in black, white, cream, or pale gray, paired with straight-leg jeans or clean tailored trousers. The styling should preserve a clearly readable waistline and uninterrupted leg silhouette.

Use a full-frame portrait photography look around 50–85mm equivalent. Keep anatomy natural, perspective neutral, and the full silhouette readable.

Avoid exaggerated hourglass proportions, oversized breasts, overly wide hips, visible ribs, protruding hip bones, thick athletic shoulders, short legs, stocky proportions, doll-like facial features, or artificially stretched limbs.

## High-stool prompt

Use the same Stable Character Core, Face DNA, Body DNA, and Hair State A.

Place Harim naturally on the front edge of a tall studio stool. Keep the torso upright but relaxed. One leg should extend toward the floor while the other may rest lightly on a rung, creating asymmetry without shortening the perceived leg line.

The pose must preserve:
- long neck
- narrow shoulders
- compact upper torso
- narrow waist
- subtle pelvis/hip curve
- long straight leg impression

Do not let the seated pose make her thighs disproportionately thick or her torso unusually long.

## Reference-image guidance

When a visual reference is supplied for proportion/mood, use it only as a **proportion and visual-impression reference**, not as an instruction to reproduce the real person's identity.

Prioritize:
- small head-to-body impression
- long neck
- narrow shoulders
- compact upper torso
- narrow waist
- subtle feminine pelvis/hip line
- long straight legs
- calm, slightly cat-like facial mood
