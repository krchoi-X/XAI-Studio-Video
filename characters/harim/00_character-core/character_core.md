## Core identity

Harim is a young adult Korean woman in her early 20s.

Her visual identity is built around a **cool, refined, slightly cat-like face** and a **tall-looking, long-lined silhouette**. She should feel elegant, slim, youthful, and quietly striking rather than glamorous or exaggerated.

The intended proportion logic is not simply “make her taller” or “stretch the limbs.” Her tall impression should come from a coordinated set of relative proportions:

- relatively small head-to-body impression
- long neck
- relatively narrow shoulders
- compact/light upper torso
- narrow defined waist
- subtle feminine pelvic/hip line
- long straight legs
- slim but healthy soft-tissue volume

Harim must never look skeletal, underweight, vertically distorted, or like an exaggerated runway-model caricature.

## Recognition anchors

Harim should remain recognizable through a combination of:

1. slightly cat-like facial geometry
2. dark defined eyebrows
3. subtly upturned almond-shaped eyes
4. refined straight nose
5. small mouth with softly full lips
6. slim clean jawline
7. small/light upper-body impression
8. long neck and narrow shoulders
9. narrow waist with subtle hip contrast
10. long straight legs
11. near-black long hair
12. recurring signature hair states, especially the high ponytail

## Drift-prevention principle

Do not interpret “tall-looking” as arbitrary vertical stretching.

The target is:
**small/light upper-body impression + long neck + narrow shoulders + narrow waist + subtle pelvis/hip line + long straight lower body.**
