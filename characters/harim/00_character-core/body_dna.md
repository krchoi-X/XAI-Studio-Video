# Harim — Body DNA

Version: 0.2

## Design objective

Harim should read as tall and long-lined because of her **relative body proportions**, not because the image is vertically stretched.

## Stable proportion logic

- tall-looking East Asian proportions
- relatively small head-to-body impression
- long neck
- relatively narrow shoulders
- compact/light upper torso
- small ribcage appearance
- slim long arms, including relatively long forearms
- narrow, clearly defined waist
- flat but soft lower abdomen
- small-to-moderate natural bust, consistent across scenes
- pelvis slightly broader than average for an East Asian woman, but not exaggerated
- graceful waist-to-pelvis transition
- subtle feminine hip roundness rather than dramatic hip width
- long straight legs
- slim thighs and calves with healthy soft-tissue volume
- lower body should look long and light, not muscular or thick

## Continuity-sensitive traits

These should remain stable across image and video scenes:

- bust proportion
- shoulder width
- waist width
- pelvis width
- waist-to-hip relationship
- thigh volume
- leg-length impression
- skin-tone family
- body-hair amount/color when visible

### Body hair
- naturally sparse
- dark brown to near-black if visible
- should remain consistent with dark hair/eyebrows

## What “slim” means for Harim

Slim does **not** mean:
- visible ribs
- protruding hip bones
- hollowed chest
- skeletal limbs
- emaciated appearance

Harim retains natural soft-tissue volume, especially around the hips and thighs.

## Drift prevention

Avoid:
- exaggerated hourglass shape
- oversized breasts
- excessively wide hips
- thick athletic shoulders
- bodybuilder abdominal definition
- short legs
- stocky torso
- unnaturally elongated femurs or shins
- vertically stretched anatomy
- generic average-height body proportions when a full-body identity shot is requested

## Core proportion sentence

When a shorter model-neutral body description is needed, preserve this invariant:

> A tall-looking, long-lined Korean woman with a relatively small head-to-body impression, long neck, narrow shoulders, compact upper torso, small-to-moderate natural bust, narrow defined waist, subtly broader pelvis with gentle feminine hip curvature, and long straight legs with slim but healthy thigh volume.
