# Harim — Face DNA

Version: 0.2

## Stable facial identity

- small, slightly elongated oval face
- subtly cat-like overall impression
- defined dark eyebrows with a clean, noticeable shape
- almond-shaped dark-brown eyes
- outer eye corners subtly raised, never exaggerated
- calm, cool, somewhat reserved gaze
- refined straight nose with a clear bridge
- small mouth
- softly full lips; naturally plump but not oversized
- slim, clean, sharp jawline
- pale neutral East Asian skin tone with realistic texture
- natural makeup, polished but restrained

## Intended impression

Harim should look:
- chic
- calm
- feminine
- slightly aloof
- memorable without appearing flashy

Avoid turning her into:
- a doll-like face
- an anime-style face
- a heavy-glamour beauty look
- a childlike or overly cute face
- a generic round-faced K-pop archetype

## Expression range

Default:
- neutral
- faint restrained smile
- quiet direct gaze
- near-direct gaze

Allowed variation:
- mild amusement
- slight concentration
- calm curiosity
- relaxed candid expression

Avoid:
- exaggerated smile
- aggressive fashion-model expression
- heavily seductive expression as a default identity cue
