# Noa Consistency / Rotation Test

Use the base appearance prompt as the identity block, then append one angle block at a time.

## Test philosophy

The purpose is not to freeze every styling detail. Hair, outfit, makeup, expression nuance, lighting, and setting may vary. The test should verify that Noa's immutable facial anchors survive angle changes, especially her eyes, nose, jaw, chin, and youthful lean midface.

## Front-facing

Photorealistic portrait, front-facing, eye-level, chest-up, neutral calm expression, plain neutral background, soft natural light. Preserve the large horizontally elongated eyes with visible sclera, straight refined nose, lean youthful cheeks, and softly rounded non-protruding chin. No dramatic styling or accessories.

## Right 30 degrees

Photorealistic portrait, head turned 30 degrees to her right, eye-level, chest-up, calm restrained expression, plain background, soft natural light. Preserve the same facial identity. The nose must remain straight and refined with no hump or aquiline curve. The chin remains softly rounded and does not project forward.

## Right 60 degrees

Photorealistic portrait, head turned 60 degrees to her right, eye-level, chest-up, minimal styling. Identity preservation is critical. Keep the nose bridge straight, with no hump or hooked tip. Keep a clean lean jawline and softly rounded non-protruding chin. The face remains youthful and lean, never gaunt or aged.

## True right profile — 90 degrees

Photorealistic true right-side profile portrait, exact 90-degree side view, head-and-shoulders framing, plain neutral background, soft directional light.

Critical profile constraints:
- forehead, brow, nose, lips, chin, and jaw must form a clean believable silhouette
- nasal bridge runs straight from brow toward the tip
- no dorsal hump
- no aquiline or convex bridge
- no hooked or drooping tip
- nose tip remains compact and natural
- chin is moderately narrow and softly rounded
- chin must not jut forward
- no shovel chin
- no underbite impression
- jawline stays clean and lean
- face remains clearly mid-20s, with no age-related hollowness

## Left 30 / 60 / 90 degrees

Repeat the same constraints mirrored to the left. Left/right differences should remain within believable natural facial asymmetry and must not change the underlying nose or chin structure.

## Loose Krea exploration prompt

Shindo Noa, an original 26-year-old Japanese woman with a lean youthful face, almost no cheek baby fat, subtle smooth cheekbones, large horizontally elongated almond eyes with noticeable visible sclera, dark straight brows, a straight refined nose, and a softly rounded non-protruding chin. She has black hair, a long neck, and a quiet resilient self-reliant presence. She looks young but not sheltered.

Preserve her core facial identity, but allow natural variation in hairstyle, outfit, makeup, expression, lighting, and urban/everyday setting. She may appear with a bob or tied-back hair, with bare or vivid red lips, in workwear, casual clothing, or homewear.

Critical identity constraints: no dorsal hump, no aquiline or hooked nose, no protruding or shovel chin, no underbite impression, no heavy jaw, no aged facial hollowness, no round doll-like eyes, no tiny pinched nose, and no generic influencer-glamour drift.

## Selection criteria

### Must pass
- recognizably the same person across angles
- large elongated eyes with visible sclera
- youthful lean face, not aged
- straight refined nose
- no nasal hump / aquiline drift
- softly rounded non-protruding chin
- quiet self-contained resilient mood

### Reject immediately
- hooked or convex nose profile
- dorsal hump
- forward-jutting or shovel chin
- underbite appearance
- aged hollow cheeks or temples
- deep lines that read older than mid-20s
- round idol/doll eyes
- generic AI-beauty drift
