## Core concept

Noa is a young Japanese woman whose face reads as youthful but unsheltered. Her identity should come from a lean mid-20s face, large elongated eyes with visible sclera, a straight refined nose, a non-protruding softly rounded chin, and a quiet resilient presence. She should feel self-made and observant rather than glamorous, cute, or hardened.

## Immutable identity anchors

### Face
- slender, slightly elongated oval face
- almost no residual cheek baby fat
- lean youthful midface without gauntness
- subtle, smooth cheekbone presence
- softly tapered lower face
- moderately narrow, softly rounded chin
- never an exaggerated V-line

### Eyes
- large, horizontally elongated almond shape
- noticeable visible sclera around the irises
- dark-brown irises of moderate size
- relatively low brow-to-eye distance
- large but never round, doll-like, or childlike

### Brows
- dark
- fairly straight
- naturally defined
- only slight lift toward the tails

### Nose
- straight refined bridge
- medium length
- narrow bridge
- compact natural tip
- no dorsal hump
- no aquiline/convex profile
- no hooked or drooping tip
- no bulbous tip

### Jaw / chin / profile
